1. Total world population
R: Show the total population of the world.

>SELECT SUM(population)
FROM world

2. List of continents
R: List all the continents - just once each.

SELECT DISTINCT continent
FROM world

3. GDP of Africa
R: Give the total GDP of Africa

SELECT SUM(gdp)
FROM world
WHERE continent = 'Africa'

4. Count the big countries
R: How many countries have an area of at least 1000000

SELECT count(*)
FROM world
WHERE area > 1000000

5. Baltic states population
R: What is the total population of ('Estonia', 'Latvia', 'Lithuania')

SELECT sum(population)
FROM world
WHERE name in ('Estonia', 'Latvia', 'Lithuania')

6. Counting the countries of each continent
R: For each continent show the continent and number of countries.

>SELECT continent, count(*)
FROM world
GROUP BY continent

7. Counting big countries in each continent
R: For each continent show the continent and number of countries with populations of at least 10 million.

SELECT continent, count(*)
FROM world
WHERE population > 10000000
GROUP BY continent

8. Counting big continents
R: List the continents that have a total population of at least 100 million.

SELECT continent
FROM world
GROUP BY continent
HAVIN SUM(population) > 100000000
